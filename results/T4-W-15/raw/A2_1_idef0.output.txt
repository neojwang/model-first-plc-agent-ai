**Arrow meanings and operating rules**

- **start** is the rising-edge event of StartCommand, TRUE for only the scan in which the button is pressed. **stop** follows the TRUE state of StopCommand.
- **h = (LevelMeter / FULL_SCALE) × h_span**, and **e = h_sp − h**.
- **settled** becomes TRUE only after **|e| ≤ eps** has remained continuously true for **10 s during regulation**. The hold timer resets when that condition is broken or regulation is inactive.
- **r** is **LOW** for h < h_target, **MID** for h_target ≤ h < h_max, and **HIGH** for h ≥ h_max.
- In step 0, **fill_valve = FALSE** and **discharge_valve = FALSE**. An accepted **start** activates regulation. During regulation, both are TRUE. **stop** has priority; **stop**, **settled**, or **r = HIGH** returns the controller to step 0, with both valve commands zero.
- **fill_valve** and **discharge_valve** are the required Boolean activity outputs, not the analog register values. While active, FillValve receives the bounded PID demand scaled to **0–1000**, and DischargeValve receives **400**. Otherwise, both registers receive **0**.
- The child-to-child links are explicit: A1 supplies conditions to A2 and error to A3; A2 supplies activity controls to A3/A4 and regulation-state feedback to A1; A3 supplies the bounded fill demand to A4.
