The model below uses **A0 as the context function**, as requested, and A1–A5 as its child functions. In strict FIPS 183 diagram notation, the context diagram is **A-0**, containing function A0; the decomposition diagram is **A0**.

Hardware tag names identify physical I/O. The required logical identifiers are preserved exactly: **start, stop, settled, r, fill_valve, discharge_valve**. In particular, `settled` and `r` are derived internally, not additional physical inputs.

**Behavioral interpretation**

- **Step 0:** `fill_valve = FALSE` and `discharge_valve = FALSE`; both physical output commands are zero.
- **Regulation:** both logical outputs are TRUE. `fill_valve` denotes PID enablement, **not** a fully open fill valve; its physical opening follows the constrained PID demand.
- **Termination:** `settled`, `r = HIGH`, or `stop` returns the sequence to step 0 and closes both valves. `stop` overrides all other conditions.
- **Settling:** the hold timer is cleared outside regulation or whenever \(|e|>\varepsilon\), so time from an earlier episode cannot satisfy a later episode’s hold requirement.
