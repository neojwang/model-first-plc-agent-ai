### Meaning of the internal arrows and controls

- **Operating-step status** identifies either **step 0 (idle)** or **the filling step**. It connects **A1 → A2** and **A1 → A3**.
- **`timer_done`** is generated by **A2** and returned to **A1**. It is an internal arrow, not a physical PLC input.
- **A1 transition rules:**
  - Initially select step 0.
  - Give `stop` priority over every other transition: whenever it is TRUE, select step 0.
  - From step 0, accept `start` as a one-scan press event and select the filling step, provided `stop` and `LSHH` are FALSE.
  - From the filling step, select step 0 when `timer_done` or `LSHH` is TRUE.
- **A2 timer behavior:** start timing on entry to the filling step; assert `timer_done` when `TimerDuration` has elapsed; reset the timer in step 0.
- **A3 output behavior:** `fill_valve` is TRUE only in the filling step; `discharge_valve` is always FALSE. The sequence decision and output update must enforce the stop and termination requirements in the same control scan.

### I/O interpretation

- `start` is the rising-edge event derived from `StartCommand` at `%IX100.0`, not its continuously held level.
- `stop` corresponds to `StopCommand` at `%IX100.1`.
- `LSHH` corresponds to `%IX100.3`.
- `fill_valve` and `discharge_valve` correspond to `%QX100.0` and `%QX100.1`, respectively.
- **`LSH` at `%IX100.2` is available but unused by this task.** Detection at 2.4 m does not terminate filling under the stated requirements. Accordingly, it is not an active functional arrow in this model; adding an `LSH`-based transition would change the specification.
