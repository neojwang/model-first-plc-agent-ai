### Meaning of the controls and internal arrows

- **Initial-step requirement:** Initialize the controller in step 0, with no active outputs.
- **Step-transition rules:**
  - Accept `start` in step 0 and enter the filling step.
  - Leave the filling step and return to step 0 when `timer_done` or `LSHH` is TRUE.
  - Whenever `stop` is TRUE, select step 0 regardless of other signals.
- **Operating-step status:** The internally retained indication of step 0 or the filling step. A1 supplies it to A2 and A3.
- **Timer activation and reset rules:** Start timing upon entry into the filling step, assert `timer_done` when `TimerDuration` has elapsed, and reset the timer outside the filling step. A2 feeds `timer_done` back to A1.
- **Step-output rules:** Set `fill_valve` TRUE only in the filling step; otherwise set it FALSE.
- **Stop-priority rule:** While `stop` is TRUE, both outputs are FALSE.
- **Discharge-inhibition requirement:** Keep `discharge_valve` FALSE throughout operation.

**Unused mapped signal:** `LSH` is available at the I/O boundary but has no specified role in this task. It therefore does not appear as a consumed functional input; in particular, reaching the 2.4 m target level does **not** terminate filling.
